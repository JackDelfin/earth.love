--------------------------------------------------
PHRASES Directory
--------------------------------------------------
Stores the PHRASE or word-path in PHRASES Directory
--------------------------------------------------

PHRASES and BRANCHES are stored similarly, each in
their own corresponding directory structure.

A PHRASE may or may not be a BRANCH, and
a BRANCH may or may not be a PHRASE.

A PHRASE is an associated collection of WORDS.

A BRANCH is like a sub-heading in the directory TREE.

The forward direction of the PHRASE is stored here
like this: (PHRASES/stored/here/like/this)
(up to 200 max characters-OS_Limitation).

--------------------------------------------------
Example
--------------------------------------------------

Life, Liberty, and the Pursuit of Happiness.

CREATES:

Directory:
--------------------------------------------------
PHRASES/life/liberty/and/the/pursuit/of/happiness

Potential Files:
--------------------------------------------------
PHRASE.dat   => life liberty and the pursuit of happiness
NAME.dat     => Life, Liberty, and the Pursuit of Happiness.
TREES.dat    => Directory.TREE; Human.Rights.TREE
TEXT.dat     => (General text for the PHRASE)
WORDS.dat    => life; liberty; and; then; pursuit; of; happiness
KEYWORDS.dat => life; liberty; pursuit; happiness
LINKS.dat    => happiness.earth; life.earth.love; liberty.earth.love

WORDS
--------------------------------------------------
The individual words from the PHRASE will have a
PHRASES.dat in the WORDS tree, containing all
PHRASES the word is on, cross-referencing the
WORDS and PHRASES.
