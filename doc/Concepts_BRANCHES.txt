--------------------------------------------------
BRANCHES Directory
--------------------------------------------------
Stores the BRANCH or (word-path) in BRANCHES
--------------------------------------------------

PHRASES and BRANCHES are stored similarly, each in
their own corresponding directory structure.

A PHRASE may or may not be a BRANCH, and
a BRANCH may or may not be a PHRASE.

A PHRASE is an associated collection of WORDS.

A BRANCH is like a sub-heading in the directory TREE.

The forward direction of the BRANCH is stored here
like this: (BRANCHES/stored/here/like/this)
(up to 200 max characters-OS_Limitation).


--------------------------------------------------
Example
--------------------------------------------------

TREE:   Animals.TREE
BRANCH: Types of Species

CREATES:

Directory:
--------------------------------------------------
BRANCHES/types/of/species

Potential Files:
--------------------------------------------------
BRANCH.dat   => types of species
DIRSORT.dat  => Override sort of the directories in this BRANCH
NAME.dat     => Types of Species
TREES.dat    => animals.TREE
TEXT.dat     => (General text for the BRANCH)
WORDS.dat    => types; of; species
KEYWORDS.dat => species (based on KEYWORD.dat in WORD)
LINKS.dat    => species.earth; species.earth.love

WORDS
--------------------------------------------------
The individual words from the BRANCH will have a
BRANCHES.dat in the WORDS tree, containing all
BRANCHES the word is on, cross-referencing the
WORDS and BRANCHES.
