--------------------------------------------------
WORDS Directory
--------------------------------------------------
Stores a WORD in the WORDS structure (w/o/r/d/s/words)
--------------------------------------------------

A word in earth.love is symbolized by several
characters or numbers, and only quote (') and
dash/hyphen (-).

The forward direction of the WORD is stored here
like this: (WORDS/f/a/n/t/a/s/t/i/c/fantastic)
(up to 200 max characters-OS_Limitation).

--------------------------------------------------
Example
--------------------------------------------------

planets

CREATES the WORDS subdirectories (in lowercase):

Directory:
--------------------------------------------------
WORD/p/l/a/n/e/t/s/planets

Potential Files:
--------------------------------------------------
WORD.dat     => planets
KEYWORD.dat  => planets
NAME.dat     => Planets
TEXT.dat     => (General text for the WORD)
TREES.dat    => solar.system.TREE
PREFIX.dat   => All words found in subdirectories
LINKS.dat    => planets.earth; planets.earth.love
BRANCHES.dat => planets.in.our.solar.system
PHRASES.dat  => planets.containing.life
PATHS.dat    => earth.planets.earth
                earth.planets.mars
                earth.planets.venus
                earth.planets.jupiter


PHRASES, BRANCHES, TREES, and PATHS
--------------------------------------------------
The individual words in the WORDS tree will be listed
on WORDS.dat in the corresponding PHRASES, BRANCHES,
TREES, and PATHS directories, cross-referencing the
PHRASES, BRANCHES, TREES, and PATHS with WORDS.
